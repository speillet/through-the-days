# Explore tags, keywords, and independent goals

## Why

The person needs to revisit themes and recognise reported actions without conflating annotations, word occurrences, interpretations, and goal progress.

## What Changes

- Deliver milestone 4 (README phase 4).
- Deliver manual tags and exact counts first, then richer matching and independent goals with transparent units.
- Keep the proposal and its tasks visibly unimplemented until verification.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- tags-and-search: Manual and approved tags, aliases, merging, and filtered rereading.
- keyword-analysis: Auditable exact, lexical-family, and semantic counts.
- goal-counters: Independent tracking modes, explicit lapse events, and retained history.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The delivered capability inventory is currently empty.

## Impact

Dependencies: build-offline-journal and add-journal-reading; preserve existing reading behavior.

Sources: README §§15–17, 8, 19–20.
