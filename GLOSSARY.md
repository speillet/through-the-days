# Through the Days

Language for a personal journal that records lived experience, reported actions, and optional guided reflection.

## Journal and reflection

**Journal entry**:
A person's writing and check-in for one chosen journal date and Morning or Evening slot.
_Avoid_: Daily report, conversation.

**Journal date**:
The civil date a person assigns to an entry, independent of when they later read it or change time zones.

**Slot**:
Morning or Evening, chosen by the person rather than inferred from the time of writing.

**Draft**:
A saved entry that has not been completed; it may contain partial writing or check-in answers.

**Completed entry**:
An entry marked complete with non-empty writing and recorded mood and energy scores.

**Check-in**:
The optional guided reflection associated with an entry: current feelings, energy, activities, pride, difficulties, and a possible small step.

**Mood**:
Self-reported pleasantness or unpleasantness at a moment, on a scale from 0 to 10.
_Avoid_: Mental health score, diagnosis, energy.

**Energy**:
Self-reported activation or energy at a moment, on a scale from 0 to 10.

**Pride**:
An optional self-reported sense of pride, on a scale from 1 to 10.
_Avoid_: Performance grade, mood.

**Perceived progress**:
An optional self-report of how much the person feels they moved forward, independent of mood, energy, and pride.
_Avoid_: Mental-health grade, objective improvement.

**Writing depth**:
A Quick Check-in, Daily Journal, or Deep Reflection presentation of the same journal entry.

**Gentle view**:
A simpler presentation that hides optional tracking and prompts while preserving the person's data and access to writing.

**Reported action**:
Something the person explicitly says they did.
_Avoid_: Intention, inferred achievement.

**Small win**:
A dated item the person explicitly recognises as meaningful, whether entered directly or approved from a suggestion. Rest is a win only when the person identifies it that way.

**Intention**:
A possible future action; it becomes reported follow-through only when the person confirms an outcome.

**Trash**:
Locally retained deleted entries that can be restored during their retention period.

## Exploration and assistance

**Tag**:
A manual annotation or an explicitly approved suggestion attached to an entry or journal date.

**Text match**:
An occurrence of a word or phrase in a chosen source, using a stated matching method.

**Interpreted event**:
An uncertain extraction from source text, with provenance and a review state.
_Avoid_: Text match, diagnosis.

**Goal period**:
An interval in one tracked goal's history, retained when a later period starts.

**Thought record**:
A guided CBT worksheet about a specific situation, thoughts, evidence, an alternative perspective, and subsequent feelings.

**AI suggestion**:
Model-authored content offered for review, separate from the person's own words.

**Approved export**:
A specific selection of aggregate information that the person has previewed and authorised to leave the private journal.
